What you put in MenuBox

Your recipes, photos, names, lists and plans stay yours. You allow MenuBox to store them and to show them to the members of the households you share them with — that is all they are used for. Reporting

To report content or a person, use Settings → About and support → Report a problem or content in the app, or write to menubox.help@gmail.com. Say what you saw and in which household. We read every report and aim to answer within 7 days.

Inside a household you can also act at once: an admin can remove a member, and anyone can leave a household (Settings → Household).

If content or an account breaks these terms, we may remove the content, or suspend or delete the account.
